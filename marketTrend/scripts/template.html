      Data

      Daily closing prices from Yahoo Finance's chart data, split-adjusted. Shares outstanding from SEC EDGAR: the cover page of each company's latest 10-Q or 10-K, or weighted-average shares where the cover page was missing. Market value = shares × close.

      Companies are the S&P 500 members listed on Wikipedia (checked weekly), with their GICS sectors. The page keeps one month of trading days; older days are dropped at each update.

      How companies are counted

      Each company is counted once with all share classes (Alphabet, Fox and News Corp each have two listings in the index). Berkshire's A and B shares are priced separately. Visa's class B and C shares are converted at approximate rates.

      Share counts are held fixed through the month, so buybacks and new issuance during the month aren't reflected.

      Left out

      Nothing.

      This is full market value, not the float-adjusted value S&P uses for index weights, so the biggest founder-held companies look a bit larger here than their index weight.
