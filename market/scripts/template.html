Data

Industry values come from the Kenneth R. French Data Library, file "30 Industry Portfolios" (built from CRSP, data through September 2026). Market value is the number of firms times average firm size for each industry, every month.

Inflation adjustment uses the BLS consumer price index (CPI-U, all items) via the datasets/cpi-us mirror. BLS did not publish October 2025 because of the government shutdown; that month is interpolated.

Coverage changes

Until June 1963 the data covers New York Stock Exchange stocks only. American Stock Exchange stocks join in July 1963 and NASDAQ stocks in July 1973, when the firm count more than doubles. Banks and insurers mostly traded over the counter before NASDAQ, so the early finance channel understates that sector.

Values are month-end. Lows inside a month, like March 2020, do not show.

Industries and sectors

The 30 industries are the Fama-French definitions, assigned by SIC code each June. Software makers like Microsoft and Alphabet fall under "Software & services"; Apple and Nvidia under "Computers & chips". The nine sector colors are a grouping made for this map.
